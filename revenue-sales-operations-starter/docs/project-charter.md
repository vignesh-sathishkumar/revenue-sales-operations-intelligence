# Project Charter

**Project:** Revenue & Sales Operations Intelligence  
**Company:** NexaCloud Solutions (fictional)  
**Role:** Business Operations Analyst (simulated)  
**Data:** Synthetic only  

## Problem statement
Sales leadership lacks a consolidated view of pipeline performance, closed-won revenue, opportunity conversion, forecast accuracy, and sales-cycle efficiency.

## Objective
Create a reproducible analysis and executive dashboard that identifies performance patterns and supports operational decisions.

## In scope
- 12 months of synthetic sales pipeline data
- Customer, product, sales-representative, opportunity, and activity dimensions/facts
- Excel validation, PostgreSQL queries, Power BI dashboard
- KPI definitions, findings, and proposed actions

## Out of scope
- Live CRM integrations
- Actual customer or employer data
- Claims of realized business impact

## Primary stakeholders (simulated)
Sales Director, Revenue Operations Manager, Regional Sales Managers, Finance Business Partner.

## Success criteria
- All key data relationships validate
- KPI calculations reconcile between SQL and Power BI
- Executive dashboard answers the five research questions
- Each recommendation has a supporting metric and explicit assumption
